Privacy Policy for Financify

Effective date: 19 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 19 September 2026
Applies to the Android application com.d7labs.financify

In short: your financial records stay on your phone.

Financify has no accounts, no adverts and no servers of ours. Your transactions and saving goals are saved in a private database on your device and are never sent to us. The only time information leaves your device is when you choose to scan a receipt: the text read from that receipt is sent to Google’s Gemini service so it can fill in the transaction form for you.

1. Who we are

Financify (“the app”) is developed and published by D7 Labs (“we”, “us”, “our”). You can contact us about anything in this policy at d7labs77@gmail.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers the Financify Android application distributed on Google Play under the application ID com.d7labs.financify. It does not cover Google Play itself, which is governed by Google’s own privacy policy.

3. Information stored only on your device

Everything you enter into Financify is written to a private storage area on your device that only this app can read. We never receive it and have no technical means of accessing it.

What is storedWhy
Income and expense transactions (title, amount, type, date, description, category) To show your balance, history and spending insights
Saving goals (name, target and saved amounts, target date, note, colour, icon) To track your progress toward each goal
App preferences, such as whether the biometric lock is switched on To respect your choices between sessions

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Camera — only when you open the receipt scanner. Camera images are processed on your device to read the text on the receipt; the images themselves are not uploaded.
  • Biometrics — if you enable the app lock, Android’s own fingerprint or face unlock is used. Your biometric data is handled entirely by Android; the app only receives a “success” or “failure” result and never sees or stores it.

5. Receipt scanning and third-party processing

When you scan a receipt, two steps happen:

  1. On-device text recognition. Google ML Kit reads the text from the camera image on your device.
  2. Receipt analysis. The recognised text is sent over an encrypted connection to Google’s Gemini API, which returns the merchant, amount, date and a short description to pre-fill the transaction form. This happens only when you use the scanner, and you can review or change the result before saving. Google processes this text under the Gemini API Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

If you never use the receipt scanner, no transaction information leaves your device.

Google ML Kit may also send limited, non-personal diagnostic and performance information (such as device model, app version and API usage metrics) to Google to maintain and improve the library, as described in Google’s ML Kit data disclosure.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not operate any server that receives your data
  • No advertising and no advertising networks
  • No Android Advertising ID
  • No user accounts, registration or sign-in
  • No connection to your bank or any financial institution
  • No payments, lending, or money transfers
  • We never sell or share your personal information

7. Device backup

If you have Android’s backup feature switched on, the app’s data may be included in your own Google account backup, like other apps on your phone. That backup belongs to you and is governed by Google’s privacy policy. We have no access to it.

8. Children’s privacy

Financify is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

Because we do not receive or store any personal data about you, we hold nothing to disclose, correct, export or erase. You keep full control of the data on your device (see section 10). You may still contact us with any request and we will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

10. Retention, and how to delete everything

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can erase it at any time by deleting individual transactions or goals in the app, by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Financify → Storage, or by uninstalling the app.

11. Security

Your records are stored in the app’s private directory, isolated by the Android sandbox, and can be protected with the optional biometric lock. Receipt text sent for analysis is transmitted over HTTPS.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app’s behaviour changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the “last updated” date above before releasing that version.

13. Contact

D7 Labs — d7labs77@gmail.com
We aim to answer privacy enquiries within 30 days.
