Privacy Policy for Financify

Effective date: 25 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 25 September 2026
Applies to the Android application com.d7labs.financify

Your financial data stays on your phone.

Financify records your income, expenses and savings goals in a private database on your own device. We do not operate any server that receives it, we have no user accounts, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. Receipt scanning runs entirely on your device.

1. Who we are

Financify (“the app”) is developed and published by D7 Labs (“we”, “us”). For the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, D7 Labs would be the data controller — although, as set out below, we do not receive or process any of your personal data.

Contact: d7labs77@gmail.com.

2. Scope

This policy covers the Financify Android application distributed on Google Play under the application ID com.d7labs.financify. It does not cover Google Play itself, which collects information under Google’s privacy policy whenever you install or update any app.

3. The financial information you enter

Because Financify is a money tracker, the information you put into it is sensitive. We have designed it so that information never reaches us. Everything below is written to a private database on your device that only this app can read:

What is storedWhy
Transactions — title, amount, income or expense, date and any note you add To show your balance, history and totals
Your income and expense categoriesTo group and filter transactions
Savings goals — name, target, amount saved, target date and note To show goal progress
Settings — theme, language, currency and whether app lock is on To keep the app the way you set it
A reference to a receipt image, if you scan one To attach the receipt to its transaction

We never receive this information and have no technical means of accessing it. There is no account to sign in to and no cloud sync.

4. Camera and receipt scanning

The app asks for camera access for one purpose: scanning a receipt so the amount and date can be read automatically. The text recognition runs entirely on your device, using a model bundled inside the app (Google ML Kit text recognition), and the recognised text is then interpreted by a parser that also runs on your device. Receipt images and the text extracted from them are not uploaded anywhere, and are not sent to us or to Google for processing. See section 7 for the one thing ML Kit does send about itself.

You can refuse or revoke the camera permission at any time in Android settings; the rest of the app continues to work, and you can enter transactions by typing or by voice instead.

5. Microphone and voice entry

If you use “Say a transaction”, the app uses Android’s own speech recognition to turn what you say into text. That is a operating-system service provided by your device and is governed by your device manufacturer’s and Google’s privacy terms; depending on your device settings it may process audio on the device or on Google’s servers. We never receive the audio or the transcript — the app only reads the resulting text locally to fill in the transaction form.

6. Biometric unlock

If you turn on App lock, Android asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock before opening the app. This is handled entirely by the Android BiometricPrompt system. The app is only told whether authentication succeeded; it never sees, receives or stores your fingerprint or any other biometric data.

7. Network access, and what does use it

The app’s manifest includes the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions. We want to be precise about why, because “this app can reach the internet” deserves an explanation in a money tracker.

We operate no server, and none of your financial records are sent anywhere. Financify contains no code that uploads your transactions, goals or settings — there is no account, no sync and no backend of ours for them to go to.

The permissions arrive with Google’s ML Kit, the on-device text-recognition component used for receipt scanning. ML Kit bundles a Google logging library (transport-backend-cct) that may send Google its own diagnostic and usage information about the library — for example that text recognition ran, and whether it succeeded. That data is Google’s, is governed by Google’s ML Kit terms and the Google privacy policy, and it does not include your receipt images, the text read from them, or any transaction you record.

8. What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising network or advertising identifier
  • No analytics, crash reporting, attribution or telemetry services
  • No user accounts, registration or sign-in
  • No selling, renting or sharing of any information — we have none to share
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, files, SMS or call logs
  • No cookies or comparable tracking technology

9. Device backup

If Android’s backup feature is switched on, app data may be included in your own Google account backup, as it is for other apps. That backup belongs to you and is governed by Google’s privacy policy. We have no access to it. You can turn backup off for this app in your device settings.

10. Children

Financify is a general-audience budgeting tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 — or the applicable minimum age in your country — because we do not collect personal information from anyone.

11. Your rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR

You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Because we do not collect or process any personal data about you, we hold nothing to disclose, correct, export, restrict or erase, and there is no processing to object to. You can still write to us and we will respond within one month, ordinarily to confirm that we hold no data about you. You have the right to complain to your national supervisory authority.

You remain in full control of the data held on your own device — see section 13.

12. Your rights under California law (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not collect “personal information” as defined by the CCPA as amended by the CPRA. In the preceding twelve months we have not collected, sold, shared or disclosed for a business purpose any category of personal information, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information. We have never sold or shared personal information and never will.

13. Retention, and how to delete everything

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Data on your device stays there until you remove it. You can delete it at any time by:

  • deleting individual transactions or goals inside the app; or
  • clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Financify → Storage; or
  • uninstalling the app, which removes its private storage entirely.

14. Security

App data is written to Financify’s private application directory, which the Android sandbox isolates from other apps. You can add a further layer by enabling App lock, so the app requires your fingerprint or screen lock each time it opens. Because your financial records are not transmitted, there is no data in transit to intercept and no server of ours to breach.

15. Changes to this policy

If the app’s behaviour changes in a way that affects your privacy — for example if cloud sync is ever added — we will update this page and the date above before releasing that version, and summarise the change at the top.
