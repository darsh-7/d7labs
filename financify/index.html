Financify

Personal finance tracker for Android · com.d7labs.financify

Track income, expenses and savings goals. Scan a receipt and the amount fills itself in — the text recognition runs on your phone, not in a cloud. No adverts, no accounts, no tracking.

Features

  • Income and expense tracking with your own categories
  • Savings goals with progress and target dates
  • Six-month income-versus-expense analysis
  • Add a transaction by typing, by voice, or by scanning a receipt
  • App lock with your fingerprint or screen lock
  • Light and dark themes, multiple languages and currencies
