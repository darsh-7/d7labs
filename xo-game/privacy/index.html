Privacy Policy for XO Game

Effective date: 11 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 11 September 2026
Applies to the Android application com.d7labs.xo

In short: XO Game collects nothing about you.

The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it is technically incapable of sending any information anywhere. There are no adverts, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no third-party software kits inside it. The handful of settings it remembers are written to your own device and stay there.

1. Who we are

XO Game (“the app”) is developed and published by D7 Labs (“we”, “us”, “our”). For the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, D7 Labs would be the data controller for the app — although, as set out below, we do not process any personal data through it.

You can contact us about anything in this policy at d7labs77@gmail.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers the XO Game Android application distributed on Google Play under the application ID com.d7labs.xo. It does not cover Google Play itself. When you download or update any app, Google collects information under Google’s own privacy policy, which is outside our control and applies regardless of which app you install.

3. Personal data we collect: none

We do not collect, receive, store on our systems, transmit, sell, rent, share, licence or otherwise disclose any personal data about you. We hold no database of users, because the app never contacts us.

This is not merely a promise about our intentions — it is a property of how the app is built. The app’s Android manifest does not declare the android.permission.INTERNET permission. Without it, the Android operating system blocks the app from opening any network connection. Anyone can verify this by inspecting the published package.

4. Permissions the app requests

None. XO Game requests no runtime permissions and no sensitive permissions of any kind. It has no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, calendar, call logs, SMS, accounts, or any device identifier.

5. Information stored on your own device

So that the game behaves the way you left it, the app saves the following in a private storage area on your device that only this app can read. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

What is storedWhy
Player names you choose to type To label the scoreboard and whose turn it is. Typing a name is entirely optional; the app works with the default “Player 1” and “Player 2”.
Chosen marks, opponent (computer or second player), difficulty, and who plays first To restore your game setup on next launch
Music, sound-effect, vibration and theme preferences To respect your choices between sessions
Your in-app language preference To open the app in the language you selected
Local win, loss and draw counts To display the scoreboard

A player name is only personal data if you choose to type one, and even then it never leaves your handset. We never receive it and have no technical means of accessing it.

6. Device backup

If you have Android’s backup feature switched on, the settings listed above may be included in your own Google account backup, in the same way as for other apps on your phone. That backup belongs to you and is governed by Google’s privacy policy. We do not have access to it, are not notified of it, and cannot retrieve anything from it. You can disable it at any time in your device settings, or by turning off backup for this app.

7. Advertising, analytics and third-party code

The app contains none of the following:

  • Advertising of any kind, and no advertising network
  • The Android Advertising ID (AAID) — the app neither reads nor transmits it
  • Analytics, crash reporting, attribution, or telemetry services
  • Third-party software development kits (SDKs) of any kind
  • Social network integrations or login providers
  • Cookies or any comparable tracking technology
  • User accounts, registration or sign-in
  • In-app purchases or payment processing

The app is built only on Google’s standard AndroidX and Material Components libraries, which run locally on the device and do not transmit data on our behalf.

8. Children’s privacy

XO Game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction, such as 16 in parts of the European Economic Area), because we do not collect personal information from anyone at all.

As the app contains no advertising, no analytics and no means of transmitting data, we believe it presents no risk of the data collection that the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Article 8 of the GDPR are designed to prevent. If you are a parent or guardian and have any question about this, please contact us at the address above.

9. Your rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, data protection law gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right not to be subject to solely automated decision-making.

Because we do not collect or process any personal data about you, we hold nothing to disclose, correct, export, restrict or erase, and there is no processing to object to. We have no lawful basis to declare because no processing takes place. Should you nevertheless wish to exercise any right, write to us and we will respond within one month, ordinarily to confirm that we hold no data about you.

You retain full control over the information stored locally on your device — see section 11. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

10. Your rights under California law (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not collect “personal information” as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act. In the preceding twelve months we have not collected, sold, shared or disclosed for a business purpose any category of personal information, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information.

We have never sold or shared personal information, and we never will. Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing to opt out of, and your rights to know, delete, correct and to non-discrimination are not engaged. You may still contact us to ask.

11. Retention, and how to delete everything

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. The settings stored on your device remain there until you remove them. You can erase all of them at any time by:

  • using Settings → Reset scoreboard inside the app to clear your scores; or
  • clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → XO Game → Storage; or
  • uninstalling the app, which removes its private storage entirely.

None of these actions requires a request to us, and none can be refused by us.

12. Security

Information saved by the app is written to its private application directory, which the Android sandbox isolates from other apps. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no data in transit to intercept and no server of ours to breach.

13. International data transfers

No personal data is transferred internationally by the app, because no personal data leaves your device.

14. Google Play Data Safety

Our Google Play Data Safety declaration states that the app collects no data and shares no data. This policy is consistent with that declaration, and both reflect the app’s actual behaviour.

15. Changes to this policy

If the app’s behaviour ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and change the “last updated” date above before releasing that version. Material changes will be summarised at the top of this page. This policy will remain available for as long as the app is published.
